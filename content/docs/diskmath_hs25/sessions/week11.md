## Notes on Last Exercise Sheet

+ The exercises this week were rather difficult, so don't be sad if you didn't get a lot of points
+ **Justify your steps! Justify your steps! Justify your steps!**
+ Avoid ...-notation, it can be dangerous.
+ The neutral elements 0 and 1 of a field have **nothing** to do with the numbers 0 and 1! They could be any mathematical objects!
+ Be careful about what is being defined in the task. Just because we defined A x B does not mean B x A is also defined.  
+ If something is not completely obvious from the definition, then you need to at least write "lemma script" as a justification (if you are using a lemma from the script).
+ Don't just write "we can now use X to show Y", show it!
